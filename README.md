Critical Mineral Explorer: From Minerals to Clean Energy

About the Project

Critical Mineral Explorer is a beginner-friendly educational website that helps users understand the importance of critical minerals in clean-energy technologies.

Many people know about electric vehicles, solar panels, and wind turbines, but they may not know which minerals are used to develop these technologies. This website explains important minerals and their applications in a simple and understandable way.

Problem Statement

Critical minerals play an important role in clean-energy development. However, many students and beginners have limited knowledge about these minerals, their properties, and their uses in renewable-energy technologies.

There is a need for a simple platform that connects information about critical minerals with their real-world applications.

Proposed Solution

Critical Mineral Explorer provides an interactive educational platform where users can:

- Explore important critical minerals.
- Learn about Lithium, Nickel, Cobalt, and Graphite.
- Understand the properties and uses of each mineral.
- Discover how minerals are used in electric vehicles, solar energy, and wind energy.
- Compare different minerals using a comparison table.
- Test their knowledge through an interactive quiz.

Key Features

- Mineral Exploration: Provides information about important critical minerals.
- Mineral Applications: Explains how minerals support clean-energy technologies.
- Comparison Table: Helps users compare minerals and their applications.
- Interactive Quiz: Allows users to test their understanding.
- Simple Navigation: Provides an easy-to-use interface for beginners.
- Educational Content: Presents technical information in simple language.
